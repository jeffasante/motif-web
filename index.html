Motif

How are these two tracks connected? Motif looks for shared recorded audio (a sample, a re-release) and for musical resemblance (a cover or interpolation). It runs entirely in this browser, using a C engine compiled to WebAssembly, so your files are never uploaded.

Where they connect

Each track's waveform, spectrogram (pitch up, time across) and chroma (the 12 pitch classes, C at the bottom). Linked bands show the passages Motif matched. Click anywhere on a track to play from there; click a band to hear the matched passage in Track A, then in Track B.

Why this result

The detectors use fixed thresholds (dashed red lines). Short, pitch-shifted or heavily processed samples and very dense mixes can fall below them, so a "no connection" result is not proof of absence. These graphs show how close each case came. Signals below are hints for a listener, not findings.
